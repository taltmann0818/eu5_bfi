# Historical banking dilemmas and charter petitions

Thirteen individually authored DHEs have three scenario-specific alternatives each. An additional Venetian anniversary event repeats the Senate’s annual mandate decision. Three generic charter petitions remain separate. Native founding events are preserved. The native South Sea Bubble DHE is extended in place and retains exactly two options.

The historical catalogue, descriptions, choices and modifiers live in `tools/generate_historical_bank_events.py`. `design/bank_events.json` contains only the generic petitions. Both generators run through `tools/generate_scripts.py`; English and all ten cached translations ship together. Translations are machine-assisted and have not received a human semantic review.

## Historical choices

All DHEs require the appropriate living chartered bank, its live controller and an operating, reconciled ownership ledger. The Italian countries must own the historical city. England includes GBR. Historical windows are inclusive, with a 5% monthly chance and once-only registration. The old shared audit/subscription/retention template is superseded.

## Native presentation and execution

Options use `show_as_tooltip` with native `add_gold`, `estate_add_gold`, `add_country_modifier`, `add_estate_satisfaction`, `add_opinion`, `add_reform`, `create_art` and other normal effects. The game supplies currency/estate/stat icons, modifier links, durations, colored values and scoped recipients. Narrative paragraphs are not substitutes for these effects. Brief custom lines explain custom bond/share ledger changes where EU5 has no equivalent native security.

The guarded execution body applies the same native effects and the necessary ledger operation once. A changed bank/controller has a neutral dismissal. An open petition blocks another new bank event. Financial choices repeat cash, capital and authority checks; stale events cannot spend from a different bank. Bank-funded office/factor expenses are booked as losses as well as reducing cash. Public gifts to reserves are transfers, not trading profit or newly issued shares.

The Giro’s winding-down flag blocks new business through the common permission trigger while retaining ordinary claim servicing. Annual reviews continue until permanence or closure. Existing saves without a first-observation date begin the three-year clock when the charter is first observed by this version. The 1666 options fund administrative/specie facilities; they do not replace BFI’s fully backed note rules with unrestricted historical fiduciary issuance.

## Generic petitions

The controller has a 2% monthly chance after a shared five-year minimum interval. Eligible weights are new subscription 40, buyback 30, retention 30. Share petitions retain existing Share Dealing, age and completed owned Stock Exchange requirements.

- **A New Subscription:** burghers pay 100 from their own estate wallet for new NAV-priced shares; existing owners are diluted. Successful payment opens subscriptions and lists the bank. Later secondary trades pay their seller.
- **The Sovereign’s Shares:** the bank buys up to 5% outstanding units from the recorded sovereign **at NAV**, retires them and releases proportional sovereign basis. Uncommitted bank cash, capital headroom, ownership revision, unchanged NAV and trading cooldown are rechecked. The native currency movements and exact quoted units/NAV are shown. Market premia/discounts do not enter the price.
- **The Dividend Must Wait:** retain prospective distributions for 24 months. The named native modifier exposes the agreement; the previous policy resumes at expiry unless a later policy supersedes it.

Consent gives +15 bank opinion and +5 points burgher satisfaction; refusal gives −20 and −5 respectively. These political changes now render as native effects. Refusal moves no cash or units. Shareholder units, claims and basis retain the existing BFI ledger rules.

## Artwork, sources and verification

Every full-scene illustration in the mod has been regenerated, including earlier founding, commenda, venture, bank-run and resolution pictures. The **31** full-scene masters in `assets_source/painted/widescreen/` are 16:9; every runtime illustration is **1920×1080 BC3/DXT5 DDS**. Architecture, minting, dock labor, construction, crowds, parliamentary and civic ceremonies, fresco painting, vaults and still lifes provide varied compositions. Full prompts and built-in imagegen provenance are in `assets_source/painted/widescreen_generation.json`. Square icons, coats of arms and portrait-shaped reform icons retain their native widget shapes.

The native 540×220 event frame is shallower than 16:9. A pinned two-line change to `eventwindow.gui` sets `fittype = centercrop` in its two custom-image slots, preserving proportions while cropping peripheral content. Removing those two lines must reconstruct the pinned native source exactly. Source validation enforces 16:9 DDS dimensions and binary headers; the exporter rejects non-16:9 masters or stretched scene exports. DDS is a binary format beginning with `DDS `, so adding a UTF-8 BOM would corrupt it. Script and localization files retain UTF-8 BOM encoding.

The chapel’s two native paintings also have substantial `<key>_desc` localization, following the installed native `artists_l_english.yml` convention. Their descriptions distinguish a Franciscan fresco cycle bearing the Bardi donors’ arms from a smaller communal altarpiece centered on civic charity. Both use a living painter and native painting quality; neither claims to be a newly created historical work by a deceased artist. The supplied [Art modding guide](https://eu5.paradoxwikis.com/Art_modding) was attempted but returned an access error; installed native definitions and localization provide the implementation evidence.

### The native South Sea Bubble

The installed event identifier is `flavor_eng.215` in `flavor_ENG.txt`. Its original title, prose, dates, triggers, two option labels, historical option marker, Walpole branch, societal-value movements, native payments and trade-company restructuring all remain. With a live chartered Bank of England, option **a** adds five years of commercial/tax contraction and −5% bank share valuation; option **b** retains its native six-month-income payment and adds five years of −5% tax efficiency, +10% bank administration expense and +5% bank share valuation. These are modifier additions only: no extra flat rescue payment, duplicate historical event, third option or standalone South Sea painting ships. Countries without the charter retain the complete native behavior.

Historical inspiration: the supplied [Bank of England history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_of_England), the Bank’s [timeline](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/about/history), the New York Fed’s account of the [1772 credit crisis](https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2022/06/the-first-global-credit-crisis/), [Bardi family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bardi_family), [Buondelmonti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buondelmonte_de%27_Buondelmonti), [Guelphs and Ghibellines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guelphs_and_Ghibellines), [San Giorgio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bank_of_Saint_George), [alberghi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albergo), [Spinola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinola_family) and [Banco del Giro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banco_del_Giro). Choices and tuning are alternate-history gameplay adaptations, not claims that each alternative occurred.

`./tools/run_campaign.ps1 -Suite bankevents` runs `tools/bank_dhe_suite.py`: the charter petition regression, unchanged authored option bodies in native hidden-event contexts, a tagged Grimaldi reform fixture, native paintings, real money and claim checks, natural monthly accounting and second-process reload. DHE calendars are bypassed in the test fixture; the war-only option is not forced in its peaceful scene. Offline checks and visual inspection are separate.
