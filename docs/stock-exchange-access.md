# Bank shares require an exchange building

At the user's request on 25 October 2026, bank-share market access requires a completed owned Stock Exchange, without Bank Share Dealing research or a Reformation age gate. An early exchange granted by an event qualifies. A chartered bank uses its live controller's exchange; an independent bank uses its own. Both specializations have the same bank-share access.

The obsolete advance is hidden and unresearchable. Its native ID and modifier remain available for old saves and explicit compatibility fixtures. Already researched advances are not refunded; they grant no trading exemption. A missing or unfinished exchange still blocks trading. No save editing or research grant is required.

The change applies to the shared access check used by admission, bank-share offers, purchases, sales and automated investment. It preserves the existing ownership, offering/inventory, cash, capital, bank operating state, diplomat, issuer cooldown and execution rechecks. It does not change separate Company Equity research or the rules for issuing new capital.
