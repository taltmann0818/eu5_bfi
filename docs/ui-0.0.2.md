# 0.0.2_dev interface revision

Credit is the shared home for state credit and bank accounts. Its native loan totals, borrowing/lending actions, filters and loan providers are retained. A managed bank adds an illustrated overview with bank cash, assets, liabilities, deposits and equity, and a shared twelve-settlement equity/debt graph. Funding & Debt combines deposits, capital and policies with state borrowing. Lending & Investments combines bank permissions and funded instruments with native lending.

Balance retains the native budget layout and inserts three sibling income cards beneath Trade Income. Dividends show only the sovereign's funded payment, excluding payments to other shareholders. Facility income shows funded receipts. Royalties use native slider artwork, minus/plus controls and estate satisfaction indicators. The five policy positions are 0%, 0.25%, 0.5%, 1% and 2% annually; the tooltip distinguishes the next assessment from the last funded transfer. These transfers already enter the treasury through settlement and are not added to native income a second time.

The slider reads the native highlight/range width ratio after layout, since this engine's public GUI API does not expose its numerical value. Its service validates the live controller and clamps input. It cannot be used by another state or an independent bank. The delay is 0.05 seconds and does not depend on campaign time. Existing schema-1 banks initialize new history and display fields without resetting their accounts.

All 18 advances have historical descriptions and technical permission tooltips. Their native effect rows use painted feature icons, while their special-advance badges use the chartered-bank reform illustration. Other native advance badges remain unchanged. Every BFI runtime icon is exported from the new original paintings or attributed community masters; see the asset register.

## Validation scope

The final runtime passed **60/60 engine assertions** and **42 offline tests** on 14 October 2026. Its sanitized result and runtime fingerprint are committed in `tests/results/2026-10-14-florence-0.0.2_dev.json`.

At 2560 × 1440, the Florence review confirmed the restored Balance labels, frames and aligned columns; the unified Credit view; opening the investment subpanel without a crash; royalty dragging to 2%, decreasing to 1%, and returning to 0%; and the Renaissance advance cards, painted permission rows, historical descriptions and chartered-bank badges. The controls are tested in disposable user directories, never a personal campaign.

The engine regression suite includes real monthly history, negative equity scaling, state dividend attribution, royalty input bounds and authorization, political modifiers, legacy display-field initialization and a second-process autosave reload. Offline checks verify source structure, deterministic generation and evidence integrity. Missing optional notification preferences remain in local logs but do not count as script execution failures.

The full resolution/UI-scale matrix, multiplayer and the previously disabled native financial bridges remain outside this revision. GitHub's engine job requires the existing licensed Windows EUV/CMF runner labels; a queued job is not a campaign pass.
