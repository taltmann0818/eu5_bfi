# Initial tuning, not campaign balance

Investment by native age: 500 / 750 / 1,000 / 1,500 / 2,000 / 3,000 gold. Treasury deposits use a chosen amount; legacy diplomatic/estate actions use 100-gold lots, 1% annual demand interest or 2% for a 12-month term. Accounts retain accepted rates; matching depositor/pool/rate/maturity lots aggregate. Estate pools use actual gold and retain a 25-gold wallet buffer. Early exit cancels accrued interest up to 1% of principal, only after explicit acceptance; renewal fixes a new 12-month term. Cash protection defaults to 25 gold plus 25% of unqueued liabilities, queued withdrawals and separately committed funds. Required equity is 10% of carrying assets.

Independent banks require +50 opinion for new accounts. The sovereign's own chartered bank adds one annual percentage point to its deposit rates. Accepted rates remain fixed. Demand interest is paid monthly when funded; unpaid interest stays owed. Term interest remains payable at maturity. Lower relations never block matured withdrawals.

Monthly fixed operating costs are 1 gold plus 0.05 per deposit account, 0.10 per contract, 0.50 per branch and 0.25 per active non-cash asset class. Reductions are additive: 5% for the State Chartered Bank reform, 10% for Banking, 15% for Advanced Clearing and 10% for a one-time 100-gold administration decision. Unpaid costs remain liabilities and suspend distributions.

The sovereign initially owns all chartered-bank shares. New subscriptions raise capital and dilute ownership at pre-transfer net asset value. Dividend presets are retain / 25% / 50% of monthly net income after current expenses and carried losses. Each holder receives owned units × funded dividends / outstanding units. Cash and capital protection can reduce the funded dividend. Retained profit does not become next month's dividend base. Autonomous takeovers are limited to one successful purchase per monthly settlement.

The [custom sovereign bond facility](sovereign-bonds.md) defaults **off**. It transfers bank cash into issuer financing with a persistent debt claim; manual and automatic issuance share a monthly cap of max(1,000 gold, six months of native state income). The manual amount picker is bounded by this remaining cap and live funding/debt capacity. Default annual coupons are 3%; new issues may use 1%, 3% or 6%. Expenses requests 60% of forecast spending, Buffer targets 200 gold, with Expenses running regardless of current cash and Buffer stopping at its target. Combined custom/native debt headroom is 24 months of native income with a 500-gold floor; custom coupons cannot exceed 25% of native income. Secondary trading requires a completed owned Stock Exchange for both parties, including buildings granted before its advance. Each bank-held custom bond adds one contract to operating costs. The planned 60%-debt-first fiscal scenario is a design target, not current measured gameplay. No random windfall, synthetic interest, automatic minting, generic income bonus or unexplained cash growth substitutes for a funded transaction.

The bank uses bounded M10 client/risk selection. Release-wide yield, funding, concentration and maturity calibration remains open; native company and banking AI is preserved.
