# Initial tuning, not campaign balance

Investment by native age: 500 / 750 / 1,000 / 1,500 / 2,000 / 3,000 gold. New accounts use 100-gold lots, 1% annual demand interest or 2% for a 12-month term. Accounts retain accepted rates. Cash protection defaults to 25 gold plus 25% of unqueued liabilities, queued withdrawals and separately committed funds. Required equity is 10% of carrying assets.

Independent banks require +50 opinion for new accounts. The sovereign's own chartered bank adds one annual percentage point to its deposit rates. Accepted rates remain fixed. Demand interest is paid monthly when funded; unpaid interest stays owed. Term interest remains payable at maturity. Lower relations never block matured withdrawals.

Monthly fixed operating costs are 1 gold plus 0.05 per deposit account, 0.10 per contract, 0.50 per branch and 0.25 per active non-cash asset class. Reductions are additive: 5% for the State Chartered Bank reform, 10% for Banking, 15% for Advanced Clearing and 10% for a one-time 100-gold administration decision. Unpaid costs remain liabilities and suspend distributions.

The sovereign initially owns all chartered-bank shares. New subscriptions raise capital and dilute ownership at pre-transfer net asset value. Dividend presets are retain / 25% / 50% of monthly net income after current expenses and carried losses. Each holder receives owned units × funded dividends / outstanding units. Cash and capital protection can reduce the funded dividend. Retained profit does not become next month's dividend base. Autonomous takeovers are limited to one successful purchase per monthly settlement.

The recurring bond facility defaults **off**, and all issuance remains rejected until P03–P05 are implemented and proved. The planned 60%-debt-first fiscal scenario is a design target, not current measured gameplay. No random windfall, synthetic interest, automatic minting, generic income bonus or unexplained cash growth substitutes for a funded transaction.

AI expansion is intentionally conservative in this first build. Comprehensive yield/risk/concentration/maturity scoring is outstanding; native company and banking AI is preserved.
