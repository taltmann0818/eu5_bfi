# M6 goods finance acceptance

Status: revised automatic all-goods M6 gate complete for the pinned single-player baseline on 19 October 2026. The former manual five-good pilot below is historical evidence. This note records the bounded product and its evidence; `coverage.md` remains the milestone register.

## Delivered product

Merchant Finance trades automatically at monthly bank close from Discovery after Commodity Investments research. The player sets the mandate (automatic, paused or run off), allocation (0%, 10%, 25%, 50% of bank cash plus owned carried inventory), risk (take-profit/stop-loss of +10%/−5%, +20%/−15% or +35%/−25%), maximum holding period (6, 12 or 24 months) and contract policy (spot only, receipt offers or forward offers). The bank chooses goods and quantities from the live native goods catalogue, including added goods. No good name or category is excluded. It ranks available stock depth by native quantity times price, avoids another position in an already held good, targets eight owned positions and opens at most four positions per month. Sixteen persistent custody records accommodate bank inventory and accepted contracts; quantities have no ten-unit cap. Each order uses up to one eighth of target capital, remaining allocation, protected free cash less a two-gold buffer, and 5% of native stock, rounded down to hundredths. Sizing scales with available bank capital and prices.

Automatic trading sells accessible free positions to reduce an allocation excess, restore free cash, run off, meet risk limits or reach the holding deadline. Pause prevents discretionary purchases and sales while storage, spoilage, security and accepted obligations continue. Origination permissions are checked again at execution. Native effects own stock and treasury movement; each acquired record stores the actual debit and stock reduction. Sales recognize only observed proceeds less remaining cost. Automatic receipt/forward listings require all age/research/collateral gates; counterparties still separately accept exact backed terms. Existing encumbered goods remain unavailable for discretionary sales.

A foreign host explicitly offers one Customs House branch; the bank's live manager accepts it. The host must own a completed native Customs House. The agreement lasts twelve months and grants custody in the host's capital market while at peace. Its separate one-gold monthly access charge moves bank cash to the host once; the mod adds no second native purchase debit or tariff. Expiry, war, building loss or insufficient free cash stops foreign access. The consenting host may freeze inaccessible custody and release the same records after renewal. A freeze creates neither cash nor new stock and does not forgive an outstanding buyer claim or security reserve.

Warehouse receipts and physical forwards require Absolutism, Merchant Finance, Collateralization and their respective advances. The bank automatically lists eligible free backing at 105% of carried purchase cost when its contract mandate permits that instrument. A counterparty chooses the bank and lot, reviews the commodity, quantity, price, fee and risk terms, and accepts separately. Quotes expire in two months; acceptance checks lot and offer generations, price, quantity, permissions, access, live parties and funding again. Receipt consideration buys ownership of the acquired goods; the bank removes its inventory asset and recognizes only the paid consideration less cost. A one-gold custody service fee is distinct income. There can be one claim per lot, and encumbered goods cannot be sold or pledged again.

A physical forward collects its entire price from the buyer and reserves equally large seller performance security from existing bank cash, while preserving the new payable's 25% liquidity buffer. Buyer principal is a payable and protected cash, not earnings. At the three-month maturity, physical delivery becomes a warehouse receipt at the surviving quantity. Missing units refund their contract-price fraction and pay the same amount from seller security. The bank releases collateral exactly once and recognizes net realized goods earnings after cost and compensation. Invalid holders, lost custody access or missing cash preserve the obligation for later servicing; they do not erase escrow. Revolutions Margining and Risk Management reserves replacement-cost increases above original seller security. A failed call attempts an early backed close; any unpayable or inaccessible close remains outstanding. These are physical contracts with cash security, not exchange-traded cash derivatives or Clearing House netting.

Receipt holders can request delivery into their capital market. Same-market delivery executes once. Movement to another market takes two calendar months, costs one gold and loses 1% of quantity at departure. This is a disclosed custody-shipment abstraction; it does not claim to create a native ship or a company trade route. The quantity remains unavailable at origin and no native purchase occurs at arrival. Native delivery sells the surviving acquired quantity into the holder's destination market and pays that holder. Broader receipt transfers require Revolutions research at the custodian and a separately accepted current-holder quote; transfer proceeds go to the seller.

Monthly storage is an explicit administrative sink of 0.1 gold per lot. The bank owes costs on its free inventory/forward backing; the accepted receipt holder owes costs on its claim. Unpaid holder costs prevent delivery. Physical stock decays by 0.167% per month (the pinned engine's five-decimal approximation of 2% per year). Cost follows actual custody ownership, principal and realized earnings stay separate, and price marks never create dividend income. Bank holders include paid receipt/forward cost in their own assets, with receipt spoilage losses and native terminal proceeds recognized at the holder. Country-owned references, generations, backing, security, payment cutoffs and shipment state persist through reload.

## Interface

Economy → Credit → Lending & Investments contains a compact Goods inventory card immediately below the stock portfolio interface. Its value shows indicative bank-owned native sale value and unrealized return. Hovering the value opens a striped, scrollable inventory tooltip containing every occupied custody record, its native goods icon/name, physical quantity, value and return. Accepted third-party receipts remain visible in custody and are excluded from the bank-owned total. A generated compass icon expands policy controls; a generated inventory chest identifies the value card. Policy choices remain in the same sub-tab. The manual buy-ten, per-lot selection, sale/list buttons and duplicate feature cards are retired. Country diplomacy retains separately accepted custody claims, host access and receipt transfers.

Marks refresh during native transaction and monthly custody servicing. They are indicative sale quotes, never dividends or booked unrealized profits; unavailable custody may prevent immediate execution. Return compares remaining quantity value with remaining paid carrying cost (holder paid basis for a receipt), and excludes separately booked storage and realized cash results.
