## Delivered behavior

| Path | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Spain and other listed bonds | An eligible bond has a buy icon on its own row. Readiness resolves the actual buyer and uses the same canonical offer requirements as settlement. Tooltip information includes principal, listing price, available buyer cash and capital. The For sale filter requires a live, current-generation, unpaid-interest-free listing. Expired AI offers may renew only if the same seller still owns the same claim and remains exchange-eligible; expired human offers close. Renewal changes no cash, principal, coupon or holder. It runs during ordinary servicing and when browsing an issuer. |
| Deposits | New deposits display Open or Closed and have an explicit controller action to change admission. Reopening requires an operating bank. The picker exposes admission/operation conditions and checks branch access; settlement retains its complete wallet, capacity and term checks. Reopening is a player action, not an automatic reversal of a past suspension. |
| Wessex purchase | At the user's explicit request, Buy shares can transfer existing treasury-held shares of the player's own operating company to the chartered bank. The bank pays the treasury at the current quote; the company receives nothing and issued shares are unchanged. This controlled-company purchase is available in either bank specialization. Foreign company investment retains its Overseas Investment/Company Equity requirements. |
| Treasury company sale | Sell shares includes the treasury's registered company holdings. The existing clearing dealer buys the sold shares and pays the treasury. Basis is released from the actual holder lots, without destroying shares, changing issued units, financing the company or crediting the bank's profit. Native confirmation identifies the seller, buyer and recipient. |
| AI share supply | An eligible AI bank owner can sell 5% of actual founder/treasury holdings to the existing clearing ledger, at most once per six months and while its opening owner retains at least 80%. Shares move from the estate or treasury to the dealer; proceeds go to that owner, not the issuer. The dealer's signed cash ledger records the liquidity credit. Automated buyers leave a 1% public float available to manual trading; manual purchases can consume it. No shares are minted. Existing AI-disable, exchange, funds, capital, holder, portfolio and company-product gates remain. |
| Dividends | Credit displays carried-loss, retention, cash and capital blockers. Company tooltips expose carried losses, payout percentage and verified-profit blockers. Losses are not forgiven and principal, deposits, share proceeds and capital contributions do not become dividend income. |

Treasury company transfers repeat the live company-control, exchange, quote, revision, cash, capital, holding-capacity and 60% bank concentration checks. Successful manual trades retain their diplomat cost and native issuer cooldown. A transfer of political control invalidates the former controller's special treasury-company path; an ordinary financial holding is preserved. Dealer company stock remains purchasable by eligible banks, including the live controller's own bank through the controlled-company permission.
