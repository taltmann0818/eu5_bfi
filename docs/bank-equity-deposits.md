# Bank ownership, income and Credit interface

The [unified implementation plan and milestone register](coverage.md) is the current requirement/status authority; this page explains the bank ownership, income and Credit revision. The asset-based royalty, its slider and political penalties are retired. Only inert legacy modifier identifiers remain so loading an old save can remove its penalties without changing wallets.

## Ownership and monthly income

A new charter assigns 100% of outstanding shares to the sovereign, including the converted bank's existing net assets. The charter investment is paid-in capital, never income. An existing charter migrates previously unidentified opening units to its sovereign while preserving explicitly registered external holdings. Repeated subscriptions from the same country accumulate in one holding. Open or close an external share offering in Credit. External banks can subscribe through diplomacy and periodically evaluate open offerings using their protected liquidity. New issuance raises bank cash and dilutes existing holders at pre-transfer net asset value.

The sovereign sets the monthly payout ratio in Credit. Its dividend is `sovereign_units * ((monthly_net_income * payout_ratio) / total_units)`, after carried losses, with funded cash and capital limits and cent rounding. All holders use the same funded payout per unit. Retained profits are closed out of the monthly profit registers so they cannot be distributed a second time next month. Each month's distribution is replay-safe.

The native bank budget's `monthly_balance` supplies the institution's native net operating result, including native lending interest and expenses. The sampler records that budget result once per period; it never moves loan interest a second time or derives earnings from an unexplained treasury delta. M4 attributes actual native payoff proceeds against acquisition cost after claim invalidation; monthly coupon observations classify, rather than duplicate, the native net budget. The persisted activity statement separates native cost recovery from earnings. Custom funded fees and bank-to-bank deposit interest join the current month's earnings. Custom deposit interest and fixed operating costs reduce profit. M2 supplies funded company-retained trade distributions, M3 supplies accepted credit and custom bond settlement, and M4 closes their bank-income cutoffs. Later goods and venture adapters retain their own gates.

## Deposits and expenses

Independent banks require their opinion of the depositor to be at least +50 when accepting a new account. The same check runs at selection and commitment. The sovereign can use Credit to deposit with its chartered bank without a relations requirement and earns an additional 1% annually. Both demand and term accounts retain their accepted rates. Withdrawals require ownership and maturity, never a continuing relations threshold. Demand interest is paid monthly from unprotected bank cash; any shortage leaves the unpaid amount in the account. Term interest is paid at maturity. Balance's Deposit Interest row aggregates funded demand interest received from all banks in the current period.

M4 adds [estate demand/term pools, matching-lot aggregation, accepted renewal and early-exit offers, per-account accrual stamps and invalid-party servicing](m4-treasury.md). Credit shows their accepted terms, unpaid interest, queued amounts and quarantine state. Native annexation transfers obligations to the legal successor; unknown recipients remain owed.

The fixed-cost schedule and reductions are in `design/tuning.json` and `docs/balance.md`. Unpaid operating expenses remain liabilities and restrict new business and dividends. Paying an old expense does not charge profit again. A sovereign can streamline administration once from Credit for 100 gold.

Hover the overview's monthly operating expense to inspect live counts and per-unit charges, the gross cost, active reductions, the rounded monthly cost and the last assessment/payment plus outstanding arrears. The contextual tooltip uses the native illustrated header, turquoise section headings and striped rows. Inactive reductions are hidden; beneficial negative percentages are green. Its bottom line graph shows up to twelve actual monthly assessments, oldest to latest, with sample hover amounts. Migration starts an empty history rather than inventing prior expenses. Monthly replay and loading cannot append a duplicate. The tooltip and accounting calculation share the same component/reduction script values; hovering does not mutate the ledger. Deposit interest and native expenses remain separate from this fixed-cost line.

## Charts and loan purchasing

The overview uses native `piechart`/`pieslice` widgets, ring textures and frames. Ownership hover opens a full striped legend with country-colored bookmark keys, percentages and owned units. The asset donut's outer ring uses distinct colors for cash, bonds, unsecured loans, secured loans, commodities and equity/ventures; its inner ring uses counterparty country colors. Hovering either ring opens the asset-type breakdown, with matching country-colored keys, carrying values and each country's percentage of total bank assets. Native debts use their actual borrower. Commodity rows use the custody market's owner; project rows use the registered counterparty. Empty or unavailable instruments are not fabricated.

Asset details are a read-only, rebuilt statement with 64 distinct counterparty rows. Exposure beyond that display bound remains represented by an explicitly labelled other/unlisted slice and tooltip row. Native debt, inventory and contract records remain authoritative. The existing ledger bounds still govern custom products. Accounting totals are independent of the display bound.

When a sovereign has a charter, Credit removes the old Loans Taken/Loans Given banner. Bank assets and liabilities belong to the bank; a compact sovereign-accounts section inside the new overview separately retains native debt, receivables and monthly costs/income, with native merged-loan breakdown hovers and empty states. The debt total retains native remaining-debt semantics, including outstanding interest; its hover separates principal and remaining interest. Native borrowing, repayment and loan lists retain the sovereign as actor. The original native banner remains for countries without a charter. The decision-installed charter reform validates its own existing installation instead of an always-false condition, so normal daily reform validation does not hide the bank UI.

Credit's Take over loan control resolves the live chartered bank before dispatch. The bank pays the native estate purchase price, and `change_loan_owner` makes it creditor of the same native debt. The sovereign does not receive the receivable or its interest directly. Banks autonomously seek at most one eligible loan per month through the same service. Both paths preserve reserve/capital checks and exclude bonds and already country-held loans.
