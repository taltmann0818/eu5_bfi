# Feature Ideas
## Core Functions
- Circular info display showing who owns the bank's outstanding shares
- Dividend payments are tied to owned shares. The sovereign starts out with owning 100% of shares once the bank is created. Issuance of more shares allows other banks to buy equity of this bank, thus diluting ownership but raising capital 
- Eliminate the royalties income item. The other income stream will be from interest earned on deposits both from the chartered bank and other independent banks. Dividends to the sovereign will handle income from the chartered bank's lending activities and entitlements from owned shares
- Diplomatic option where a sovereign can deposit their money with other independent banks to earn small amounts of interest on it. Can do the same with their chartered bank via the Credit tab and earn better interest on their deposits with their chartered bank. 
- Dividends earned by sovereign from shares are determined by monthly dividend payout ratio set by the sovereign in the Credit tab; monthly dividend payout to sovereign then gets calculated as number of shares owned by sovereign * ((total monthly chartered bank net income * payout ratio) / # shares outstanding)
- The Bank should have fixed monthly operating expenses scaled by complexity of the bank; various reforms, advances, and decisions by sovereign can reduce this expense
- Most automation shouldn't be needed because the bank will be taking actions as its own entity on its own like writing loans and building things to remain solvent.
- Modify the functionality of the "Take over loan" button. The chartered bank should perform this action on their own to take over loans from other nations. But the player can also do this on their own by using this button. Once completed with another sovereign's loan, that loan should transfer the bank's balance sheet and interests payments as receivables to the bank not the sovereign. Make sure all the tooltips and information on the Credit screen reflect this.

## Future Content
- Laws affecting the bank: usury, ...
- Catholic Church (international org) laws on usury
- Banking leagues as international organizations to facilitate and consolidate the global financial systems 
- Crises: run on the bank when lots of depositors come running to the bank, requiring immediate liquidity from the sovereign; credit crisis where lots of bad loans default creating a liquidity crisis and potential insolvency
- stock exchange building/advance opens up the sovereign (including AI) participating in the secondary equities market previously only allowed for banks. Allows purchasing other bank’s shares to earn dividend revenue (no chartered bank required). converts their own chartered bank shares into preferred shares that are worth more in the dividend calculation 
- all banks will activity try to avoid insolvency. but if they start facing a liquidity crisis, they will be willing to accept increasingly worse terms at lower interests rates that countries can take advantage of
