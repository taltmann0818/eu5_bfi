# Feature Ideas
## Core Functions
- Don't gate secondary trading behind both the advance and stock exchange building, but just the building. This is because some events grant the building early and countries should be able to access the secondary market once the building is constructed pre-advance.
## Future Content
- Laws affecting the bank: usury, ...
- Catholic Church (international org) laws on usury
- Banking leagues as international organizations to facilitate and consolidate the global financial systems 
- Crises: run on the bank when lots of depositors come running to the bank, requiring immediate liquidity from the sovereign; credit crisis where lots of bad loans default creating a liquidity crisis and potential insolvency
- stock exchange building/advance opens up the sovereign (including AI) participating in the secondary equities market previously only allowed for banks. Allows purchasing other bank’s shares to earn dividend revenue (no chartered bank required). converts their own chartered bank shares into preferred shares that are worth more in the dividend calculation 
- all banks will activity try to avoid insolvency. but if they start facing a liquidity crisis, they will be willing to accept increasingly worse terms at lower interests rates that countries can take advantage of
- Random decisions where bank wants to issue shares
- New buildings and branch locations
